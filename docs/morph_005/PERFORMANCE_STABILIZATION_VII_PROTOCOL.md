explicitly supplied outward route whose sampled intermediate/endpoints are clear

not spontaneous disengagement. Geometric interlocking/mechanical impossibility

INDETERMINATE. Numerical imprisonment requires rejection of such a verified
available escape, with pair/other obstacles separately accounted for.

emission rejected separately. Geometry integrity means canonical mesh construction

and is not automatically malformed. No aesthetic acceptance is inferred.
