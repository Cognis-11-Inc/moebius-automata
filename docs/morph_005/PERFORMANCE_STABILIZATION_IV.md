## Procedural geometry ownership and remaining limits

`Filament.nodes` retains the ordered paid centreline, position and deposit tick;
`PipeSection` retains inner/outer radii and start/end seals. Wall thickness is
outer-minus-inner. Orientation and tangents are reconstructed by
`filament_chunk_at`, not a persisted frame per section. Its cubic interpolation,
three interior samples per edge, elliptical section, ridges, nozzle wobble and
age-dependent radii define the actual triangulated surface. End-cap inner centres
shift by wall thickness/edge clearance. Junction records link existing paid nodes;
they do not authorize filling arbitrary gaps. Treating these shapes as ideal
circular annuli would therefore change geometry unless used only as proven
conservative bounds. No such replacement was adopted. Existing horizon spheres
already reuse procedural bounds; the accepted new BVH pruning reduces actual
triangles without changing this source geometry.

The constructor's founder guard is1..=16, and HabitatRules::validate separately
bounds max_agents to16 (default8). Additional founders are paid from condensate
and placed canonically. Extending the budget requires changing both contracts,
checking placement/material budgets and new scaling evidence. No >16 fixture
was manufactured. The five population scenes follow different trajectories and
structures; their timings alone cannot establish an asymptotic complexity law.

The inspected runtime has no configurable world extent or dense empty-volume
allocation. The spatial example's A labels are historical translation labels,
not actual habitat resize controls. Actual1x/10x/100x extent experiments are
NOT SUPPORTED without adding/changing physical configuration, which this slice
does not authorize. Position normalization/relative transforms are constant work
per active object/query. Sparse cells and occupied geometry dominate scaling.

Memory limits apply to the new resident history and existing per-runtime caches,
not every project allocation. In particular the pre-existing process-wide
presentation_slack definition cache has no eviction policy. This slice defers its
use but does not establish unlimited-definition or whole-engine memory stability.
