## 0. FUNDAMENTAL DIRECTIVE

HABITAT-003 represents a deliberate expansion of the MŒBIUS AUTOMATA research programme.

We are no longer restricting our investigation to computational techniques commonly employed in video game development.

We shall investigate the scientific methods developed to simulate physical reality across enormous spatial, temporal and organizational scales.

Our research territory now includes computational astrophysics, fluid dynamics, atmospheric science, plasma physics, oceanography, molecular dynamics, computational biology, materials science and high-performance computing.

The ambition of MŒBIUS AUTOMATA shall not be reduced merely because its realization exceeds the capabilities of its current architecture.

**Current computational feasibility is not a prerequisite for scientific exploration.**

However:

- Experimental evidence remains mandatory.
- Numerical approximations must be explicit.
- Conservation failures must remain visible.
- Architectural limitations must be documented.
- Negative results must be preserved.
- No scientific claim may be promoted beyond its demonstrated evidence.
- No experimental architecture becomes canonical without explicit Operator authorization.

The purpose of this milestone is not to defend D3Q19, the existing grid architecture, the global timestep or any previous implementation decision.

The purpose is to discover which computational architectures deserve to survive.

## 4. RECONSIDER THE COMPUTATIONAL FOUNDATIONS

Investigate the following architectural hypotheses.

**H1 — Spatial adaptivity**

A physically nonuniform computational resolution may preserve relevant atmospheric phenomena while reducing unnecessary fine-grid computation.

**H2 — Temporal adaptivity**

Physical regions and processes may evolve at different lawful update frequencies without losing causal continuity.

**H3 — Conservative hierarchical coupling**

Multiple spatial and temporal resolutions may exchange matter, momentum and other conserved quantities through explicit synchronization contracts.

**H4 — Native hierarchical authority**

A hierarchy that owns its physical state directly may avoid the dense encoding, decoding and validation costs exposed by HABITAT-002.

**H5 — Alternative physical solvers**

A conservative finite-volume solver or another scientifically justified method may provide a more suitable atmospheric foundation than the existing Lattice Boltzmann representation.

**H6 — Hybrid physical representations**

Different physical regimes may require different representations, including continuum fields and statistical particles.

**H7 — GPU-resident environmental computation**

A complete resident environmental transaction may reduce execution costs more substantially than isolated GPU carrier kernels.

**H8 — Physically driven refinement**

Local physical indicators may govern computational refinement independently of rendering, camera position or Witness activity.

All hypotheses begin UNRESOLVED.

## 12. REDEFINE THE NUMERICAL EXACTNESS CONTRACT

Different numerical resolutions cannot generally preserve identical microscopic state.

HABITAT-003 shall establish separate qualification categories.

### Category I — Exact implementation equivalence

For optimized implementations of the same numerical model and discretization:

- Exact state comparisons where required by the existing contract.
- Exact deterministic replay.
- Identical commit semantics.
- Identical authoritative inventories.

### Category II — Numerical model equivalence

For different spatial resolutions, timestep schedules or discretizations:

- Preregistered physical error tolerances.
- Quantified approximation error.
- Convergence studies.
- Conserved quantity accounting.
- Validated stability.
- Physical observable comparisons.

### Category III — Model-form comparison

For substantially different governing equations or physical closures:

- Explicitly documented differences.
- Identification of overlapping validity regimes.
- Appropriate physical reference problems.
- No claim of equivalence beyond demonstrated observables.

### Category IV — Exploratory methods

For incomplete or unqualified scientific experiments:

- No canonical authority.
- No production claims.
- Preserve raw evidence and limitations.
- Record the next experiment required.

Deterministic replay must remain meaningful within each declared numerical representation and execution contract.

Cross-device bitwise equivalence must be demonstrated independently rather than assumed.

Numerical approximation is authorized.

Undocumented loss of physical state is not.
