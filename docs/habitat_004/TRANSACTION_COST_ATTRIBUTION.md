# Transaction cost attribution

The original scientific Profile is reused: clone, topology, numerical kernel and
final validation, plus full outer transaction. Fresh archived003 shear32 results
are in BASELINE_STAGE_ATTRIBUTION external evidence and the protocol. Other unchanged
baseline checkpoints reproduce exactly; new timings remain separate historical windows.

Persistent topology Workspace holds sealed geometry identity (ordered keys,roots,
h bit pattern,periodicity) and derived faces only. Payload/source changes do not
alter geometry. Identity mismatch rebuilds globally; no local invalidation or
incremental graph construction is implemented. Cache identity inspection/allocation
is included in outer transaction and its topology timer. Face buffers are immutable
once generated. They have no material authority or checkpoint/history ownership.

SSP2 numerical-stage time includes both Euler evaluations and intermediate full
validations. Its final-validation timer is not the total validation cost. Cached
and rebuilt first-order/higher-order pairs preserve operation order; exact full
digests/payloads are the required comparison. Deliberate invalidation bypass,
negative reconstructed density and missing neighbor tests expose failure and
preserve the old committed state. Final latency/variation follows measured windows,
not anticipated speedups. Hardware counters were not collected: perf unavailable.
